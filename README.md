### About the Experiment 

The virus growth curve experiment is performed to study the replication pattern of a virus inside a host cell over a period of time. Since viruses are obligate intracellular parasites, they can multiply only inside living cells. This experiment helps in understanding the different stages of viral replication and the time required for the production of new viral particles. The one-step growth curve is characterized by synchronous infection of host cells using a high multiplicity of infection (MOI) and periodic measurement of viral titer.
